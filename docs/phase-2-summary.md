# Phase 2 – Simple Plan

Plain-English summary of `phase-2-proposal.md` for the owner (ELM-P2-PLAN),
written after the owner found the full proposal too technical. The same
content is attached to the Trello card as a Word file.

## What we need from you

Quickest reply: "Agree with all". Otherwise give the number and your answer.

| # | Question | Our suggestion |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Who approves a leave request? | The employee's manager; Admin if there is no manager. |
| 2 | Can staff take half days? | Yes, morning or afternoon. |
| 3 | Should public holidays not count as leave? | Yes; Admin enters them once a year. |
| 4 | Which email address sends system emails? | A company no-reply address (owner to name it). |
| 5 | Can employees see teammates' leave? | Not for now; managers and Admin only. |
| 6 | How long is leave history kept? | At least 7 years. |
| 7 | Leave out carry-forward for now? | Yes. |

## Phase 2 in 4 stages

| Stage | What you get | Rough time |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Get ready | Finish Phase 1, connect email, managers and departments, change history | 1–2 weeks |
| 2. Everyday use | Manager approvals, holidays, half days, leave types, notifications | 2–4 weeks |
| 3. See the big picture | Team calendar, reports, Excel download | 1–2 weeks |
| 4. Keep it safe | Tested backups, running guide, Jarvis reliability | 1–2 weeks |

About 6–9 weeks in total (27–42 developer-days, see the full proposal).
Dates follow once the questions are answered. Cards are created after the
owner replies, and none is started until the owner says go.
